    Method

    Takt is net available time per day divided by daily demand. Any process whose effective cycle time is above takt cannot keep up, regardless of how much WIP sits in front of it.

    Inventory days are WIP divided by daily demand. Lead time is their sum plus processing time. In the current state that queue time is almost all of it.

    Value-added counts only steps that change the part's form or fit. Receiving, inspection, packing and shipping are necessary but non-value-added. Toggle VA on any card to test a different rule.

    PCE is value-added time over lead time. Working-time basis uses net shift time per day; the calendar basis uses 24 h and gives the lower number most posts quote.
